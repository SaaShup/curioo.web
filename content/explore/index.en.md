## Explorer Rules

# Join the Adventure with **Curioo.city**! 🎮🌍

## Welcome, Explorer! 🌟  
Your mission, should you choose to accept it, is simple:  
**Dive into curioo.city to uncover hidden treasures, complete thrilling circuits, and win incredible rewards.**

---

### 🚀 **Your Adventure Starts Here:**
1. **Choose your starting point:**  
   Select a **🌀 Portal in the world** and enter a guide’s route.

2. **Choose your starting point inside the portal :**  
   Select a **🧰 Chest in the world** and start moving toward it.  
   <span style="color:#f39c12;">🌟 Tip: Take a strategic route to maximize your rewards!</span>
   
3. **Explore and Discover:**  
   Once you reach the Point, open the **Treasure** waiting for you and collect your **Tokens**!  
   Each treasure is unique and can only be discovered once.

4. **Continue Your Journey:**  
   The **next Point** in your Circuit will be automatically suggested. With each step, you'll get closer to unlocking even **greater rewards.**

- **Have fun** exploring fascinating and exciting locations.

- 🐾 <b>CuriooCoins:</b><br/>
Use them to buy unique in-game items, spend them in the Guide’s local shop, or upgrade your cards!

- 📜 <b>Quests:</b><br/>
There are many 📜 <b>quests</b> to complete. Some are easy, others are more challenging. For example, there are quests to open 🧰 chests, travel certain distances, or collect specific types of 🎴 cards.
The most common one is the daily 🧰 chest, which appears once a day and allows you to discover a new 🎴 card.

   The most important 📜 <b>quest</b> is to <b>upgrade</b> 🔀 your 🎴 cards. When you own a card and are at its location, you can 🔀 <b>transform</b> it.

<div style="color:#f39c12; margin-left: 1.5rem;">

   You own the Place Stanislas card and travel to the coordinates shown on the 🎴 card.<br/>
   You can then make this card golden for <b>10 CuriooCoins</b> to prove to everyone that you have been there!<br/>
   If you do not own the 🎴 card, you will need to pay <b>40 CuriooCoins</b>!

- **🎴 Guide Cards:**<br>
Look for **🎰 Golden Machines** in 🌀 portals to obtain **🎴 Guide Cards** that are already golden for <b>10 CuriooCoins</b>.

   **🎴Guide Cards** are created by guides and correspond to the places you visit.

   If some world cards you found in chests are too far away to be golded, focus on **🎴 Guide Cards** to progress faster in your collection!
